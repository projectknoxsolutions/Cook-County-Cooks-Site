Troubleshooting

Failed Instantly ▼

Usually wrong Account or wrong PIN. Check if "Number Lock" (VZ), "Line Lock" (Visible), or "Port Protection" (TM) is active.

Stuck Pending ▼

Google Voice numbers must be manually UNLOCKED at google.com/voice/unlock. VOIP ports can take 24-72 hours.

Tracfone / Hard Ports ▼

Text IMEI to 611611 for account #. Text NTP to 611611 for PIN. Corporate ZIP: 33178.
